Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

IOctane's Google API integrations are personal automation tools built and used solely by IOctane's founder, for IOctane's own internal workflows. They are not distributed to, or used by, any other person or organization.

Where an integration requests access to Google services (for example, Google Drive), that access is used only to read or write files within the founder's own Google account, as part of a specific, narrowly scoped automation (for example, organizing personal notes into Google Docs). No data obtained through these integrations is sold, shared, transferred to any third party, or used for advertising.

Any credentials or tokens issued for this access are stored locally, on hardware controlled by IOctane's founder, and are not transmitted to any other party.

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello-world@ioctane.dev.
